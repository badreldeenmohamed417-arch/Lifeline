# 🚨 Lifeline

> **One action. One signal. Help when it matters most.**

Lifeline is an Android emergency application designed to help users quickly alert trusted contacts during an emergency.

With a single emergency trigger, Lifeline can send a customizable emergency message, share the user's current location, and provide a fast fallback to emergency services.

---

## ✨ Why Lifeline?

During an emergency, every second matters.

Opening a messaging app, finding a contact, typing a message, and explaining your location can be difficult or impossible under stress.

Lifeline reduces the process to one simple action:

```text
Press & Hold for 2 seconds
          ↓
      🚨 Lifeline
          ↓
   Get current location
          ↓
 Send emergency SMS
          ↓
 Open emergency call
